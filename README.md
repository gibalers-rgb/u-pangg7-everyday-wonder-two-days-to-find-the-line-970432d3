One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment
strange and new — an entry for the make.fun bounty **Everyday Wonder, Two
Days to Find the Line**.

## The entry

> My phone charger only works when the cable bends exactly where it broke, like it is asking me to hold its hand.

111 characters, one sentence.

## Why this angle

Everyone who has owned a dying charger knows the ritual: wiggle the cable,
find the one angle where it connects, hold it there. The sentence keeps
that real, specific moment — then turns it. The charger is not broken
hardware; it is a small needy creature asking to have its hand held, and a
daily annoyance becomes a quiet act of care. The surprise lands in a single
read, and every word works.

## How it runs

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) validates the
sentence — one sentence, 1–200 characters, a single line — and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`, where the bounty gate scorer checks it.
